Where markets stand

The S&P 500 is 3.3% below its 6 October record. Under the surface, technology, gold, silver and bitcoin are already in deep falls while energy, value shares and staples sit near highs.

Warning signals, tested on history

Hit rate is the share of months with the signal on that were followed by a 20% fall in the S&P 500 within a year. Plain odds are what you get with no signal at all. No single signal beat a coin flip.

The line to watch: the S&P 500 ending a month below its 10-month average (7,302) while valuation stays in its top 10%. That pairing was followed by a 20% fall within a year half the time. It is off today.

What worked during a fall, and after the low

The hedges that paid during a fall usually lost afterwards. What fell most rebounded most, if it survived.

  • Government bonds paid when inflation was low (2000, 2008, 2011, 2020) and failed when inflation was the cause (1973-74, 2022, March 2026).
  • The US dollar rose against the Canadian dollar in 9 of 12 falls since 1980 and was flat in the other three.
  • Company rows cover survivors only. Lehman, Enron and Nortel are not in the data.
