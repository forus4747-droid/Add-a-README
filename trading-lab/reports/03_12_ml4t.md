## Problems found
1. **No factor has a t-stat above 1 on VAL**; signs flip between 2024 and 2025 for most.
2. **The boosted model stopped after 1 tree** — the 2023 check set got worse with any further training.
3. **"Excess return" without IC is a trap.** Parkinson volatility shows +18%/yr excess on VAL but +0.0% on
   DEV with negative IC: owning the most volatile stocks paid in 2025's rally. That is market exposure, not
   prediction. (Same pattern explains Qlib's +15.9%.)
4. The repo's promised "101 formulaic alphas" are not in the code — only cited in a README.
5. The repo's own feature library (`ml4t.engineer`) is not installed; formulas were ported to pandas.

## Verdict
**Excellent teaching material, no ready-made predictive edge on 2025.** Keep: its factor definitions as a
feature library, and its insistence on time-split validation.
