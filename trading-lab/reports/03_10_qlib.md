## Problems found
1. **The model learned almost nothing:** early stopping halted at tree 9 — after that, more training made
   2023 predictions worse. The 158 price/volume features carry little stable signal for 5-day US returns.
2. **+15.9%/yr top-quintile excess on VAL with t = 0.96** is not evidence of skill; DEV excess was +1.1%.
   Most likely a tilt toward volatile stocks in a rising year (see the same pattern in Step 3.12).
3. Qlib crashed after training because of its experiment logger (MLflow file store refused); logging was
   redirected. Bug re-run, same seed, same result.
4. Our data has no VWAP; (high+low+close)/3 was substituted for that one input.
5. Benchmark hyper-parameters are tuned for Chinese stocks and 1-day labels.

## Verdict
**No proven predictive edge out of the box.** The framework itself is sound (no look-ahead found; features
reproducible). Keep the pipeline design (features → model → strict time split); the default model is not a predictor here.
