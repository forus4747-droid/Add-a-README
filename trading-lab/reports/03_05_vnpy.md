## Problems found
1. **The `vnpy` package alone cannot backtest.** The backtester is a separate package
   (`vnpy_ctastrategy`), which had to be installed, and it imports a GUI toolkit; it only loads on a server
   with `QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen`.
2. **No market orders in the backtester.** Only limit orders, filled on the next bar at
   `min(limit, open)`. "Buy at next open" has to be faked with an absurdly high limit price (1e7).
3. **Share count is fixed before the fill price is known.** The adapter changes the order's volume inside
   the `on_order` callback when the next bar arrives. Reviewer note: this works and has no look-ahead (the
   open is the fill price itself), but it mutates an engine object in an undocumented way. Without it,
   sizing from the signal bar's close differs on 120 of 9,304 entries (1.3%).
4. **No per-trade commission or P&L.** The engine reports fees only as daily totals. Per-trade `pnl_net` is
   computed from the engine's fills with the protocol formula; the worker cross-checked one symbol against
   the engine's daily total (AAPL S1: −4373.0337 both ways).
5. **Cannot close at the last bar's close.** Extra closing bar needed (same as backtesting.py).
6. The engine expects a database; bars were injected directly into `engine.history_data`.

## Verdict
**Arithmetic exact, but a poor fit for US stocks.** Every one of the five workarounds exists because the
tool is built for Chinese futures workflows. Fast (11 s for S1). Not recommended for the final repo: it
adds nothing vectorbt does not do with less risk.
