# Proof Before Build

An evidence review of four online business models, compiled against a hard
constraint: **~$100 of capital, unlimited time, AI fluency as the only skill.**

The question asked was "give me a proven system." This repo is the answer to the
prior question — *what does the evidence actually support?* — because three of the
four models under consideration do not survive their own data.

**Read it:** [`proof/index.html`](proof/index.html)

## Verdicts

| Model | Verdict | Load-bearing figure |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed-scope productized service | **Supported** | Solo operators $50k–$300k ARR; multi-year public dashboards |
| AI services, sold as an outcome | **Conditional** | 80%+ of SMBs who paid report gains — but only if never framed as "AI" |
| Digital product / template shop | **Ruled out** | Median creator earns $72/month; 99.5% of revenue to top 1% |
| Commodity AI writing & design | **Ruled out** | −5.2% writer earnings, −9.4% designer earnings post-ChatGPT (n=92,547) |
| Content / affiliate site | **Ruled out** | 12–24 months to income; fails the cash constraint |

## The four constraints that survive all the evidence

1. Sell a **service**, not a product.
2. Sell to **businesses**, not consumers.
3. Sell an **outcome**, never "AI" — only 17.7% of US small businesses have ever paid for an AI tool.
4. Sell to **boring offline niches**, not tech — ~5× the cold-email reply rate, and the saturation isn't there.

Nothing in the evidence supports anything passive, or anything that skips outreach.

## The test

No document can prove a model works for a specific person. The dossier ends with a
falsifiable experiment instead: **$39, ~3 weeks, 100 emails to one narrow niche,**
with kill criteria fixed in advance so they can't be rationalised away afterwards.

## On the quality of this evidence

Every figure carries a confidence tag — `STRONG` (peer-reviewed or official),
`MODERATE` (credible report, primary source unopened), `WEAK` (single unverifiable
secondary source). Primary documents could not be opened directly from the compiling
environment, so most figures are as-reported via search summaries.

The dossier also documents where its own strongest evidence is weakest: every public
revenue dashboard cited is a **survivor**, and the denominator — how many ran the same
play and got nothing — is not collected by anyone.
