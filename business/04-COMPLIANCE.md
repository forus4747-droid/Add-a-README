# Legal & Compliance

Not legal advice. These are the rules that get people's domains blacklisted and their
businesses fined. Read the one for where your **prospects** are, not where you are.

## United States — CAN-SPAM

Cold B2B email is **legal**. Requirements:

- [ ] A real physical postal address in every email (a PO box or registered agent address is fine)
- [ ] A working opt-out — "reply STOP" counts
- [ ] Honour opt-outs within **10 business days**, then never contact again
- [ ] Truthful subject line and truthful "From" name
- [ ] No harvested or scraped-in-bulk address lists

Penalty: up to ~$53,000 **per email**. It is enforced.

## UK — PECR + UK GDPR

- Cold email to **corporate bodies** (Ltd, LLP, PLC) is permitted.
- Cold email to **sole traders and most partnerships** is treated as an individual — you need consent. **Many small clinics are sole traders. Check Companies House before sending.**
- Identify yourself, give an opt-out, honour it immediately.

## EU — GDPR + ePrivacy

Stricter, and it varies by member state. You need a **legitimate interest** basis,
documented before you send, plus a privacy notice and immediate opt-out. Germany and
Italy are the strictest. If your prospects are in the EU, get local advice before a
volume campaign.

## Canada — CASL

The strictest of all: consent generally required **up front**. Narrow business-relationship
exemptions exist. Penalties up to CAD $10M. Do not run a cold campaign into Canada without advice.

---

## Regardless of jurisdiction

- **Never scrape emails in bulk.** Hand-collected, individually verified, publicly listed business addresses only.
- **Never send from a shared or bought domain.** Your own domain, warmed.
- **Honour every opt-out the same day.** Keep the suppression list forever.
- **One follow-up maximum.**

## Client-side risk, once you have paying clients

You are publishing under someone else's name. That carries real exposure:

- **Never publish clinical or personal details** about a reviewer. In healthcare niches this
  can be a HIPAA (US) or GDPR (UK/EU) breach *by your client*, caused by you.
- **Never admit fault** on a negative review — it can be used in a complaint or claim.
- **Never respond to anything alleging malpractice, injury, or discrimination.** Flag it to
  the client and let them or their lawyer handle it. Put this in writing.
- Get written approval on the first batch for every new client before anything goes live.

## Put these in your terms

1. Client approves all replies before first publication.
2. You do not respond to reviews alleging harm, malpractice, or legal violation.
3. Client is responsible for factual claims about their own services.
4. Either side can cancel monthly with 30 days' notice.
5. No guarantee of rating improvement, ranking, or revenue. **Never promise a number.**
