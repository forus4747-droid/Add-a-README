## Why this offer specifically

- The prospect list and the pain are **the same dataset** — Google Maps shows you who
  has unanswered reviews before you contact them.
- The free sample **is the product**, and costs ~15 minutes to personalise.
- It **renews by nature** — reviews keep arriving.
- The buyer never has to hear the word "AI". They are buying replies to reviews.

## Before you send anything

1. Replace every `REPLACE` placeholder in `site/index.html`.
2. Buy a domain and warm it for 10–14 days. Skipping this measures your spam score, not your offer.
3. Read `04-COMPLIANCE.md` for wherever your **prospects** are.
4. Write the kill criteria from `05-RUN-THE-TEST.md` somewhere you'll see them in week three.

## The honest expectation

100 emails → 3–6 replies → 1–3 sample requests → **0–1 paying clients.**

One round may produce zero. That is a normal result, which is why the kill criteria run
to 300 emails before you abandon the offer. Budget for three rounds.

Success is **one paying client** — not ten, not $10k. One person who sent money for work
you delivered. Everything after that is repetition.
