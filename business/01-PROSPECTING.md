# Building the 100 — free, by hand, ~4 hours

Small hand-built lists reply at **5.8%**. Bought lists and big blasts reply at **2.1%**.
The manual work IS the edge. Do not automate this away.

## Step 1 — Search

Google Maps: `"dental practice" <your city>`. Work outward suburb by suburb.
Aim for a 40-mile radius so you have 200+ to filter down to 100.

## Step 2 — Record each prospect

Log into `tracker.csv`. For each business, capture:

- Business name, owner/practice manager first name (from the About page or Google)
- Email — check website footer → Contact page → About page → their Facebook page
- Total review count
- **Unanswered review count** ← the qualifying number
- Star rating
- One specific detail you can reference (a recent 5-star review, a service they just added, a new location)

## Step 3 — Where to find the email

In order of what actually works:
1. Website footer
2. `/contact` page
3. Facebook page → About → Email
4. WHOIS on their domain (often the owner's personal address)
5. Guess and verify: `info@`, `hello@`, `frontdesk@`, `firstname@` — check with a free verifier before sending

**Never use a contact form.** It goes to a receptionist and dies.

## Step 4 — The 10

Pick the **10 with the worst unanswered backlog** — 30, 40, 50 unanswered reviews.
These are your free-sample targets. Their pain is most visible and the sample lands hardest.

## Hard rules

- **Never buy a list.** Bought data is stale, unqualified, and gets your domain burnt.
- **Never send to `noreply@` or generic role addresses at franchises.**
- **Stop at 100.** More prospects is procrastination. Send, then judge.

## Time budget

| Task | Time |
|---|---|
| Find and qualify 100 prospects | ~4 hours |
| Produce 10 free samples | ~2 hours |
| Write the 100 personalised opening lines | ~2 hours |
| **Total before a single reply** | **~8 hours** |
