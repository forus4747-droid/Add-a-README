# The Offer

One niche. One deliverable. One price. Do not deviate until the test says to.

## What you sell

**"Review Rescue" — a done-for-you Google reputation service for local clinics.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Deliverable** | Every unanswered Google review replied to, in the owner's voice + their Google Business Profile rewritten + 4 Google Posts scheduled |
| **First month** | $497 one-off (clears the entire backlog) |
| **Then** | $297/month (all new reviews replied within 24h + 4 posts/month) |
| **Your time** | ~3 hours for the backlog, ~40 min/month after |
| **Their alternative** | A local agency quoting $1,500/mo, or nobody |

## Why this offer and not another

- **The prospect list and the pain are the same dataset.** Google Maps shows you the business, the email, the review count, and — critically — whether the owner has replied. You can see who needs this before you contact them. Almost no other offer has that property.
- **The free sample is trivially personalised.** You can draft 10 real replies to their real reviews in 15 minutes. That is not a pitch, it is the product.
- **It renews by nature.** Reviews keep arriving. The work never finishes, so neither does the invoice.
- **Nobody has to understand AI.** They are buying replies to reviews. How they got written is not part of the conversation.
- **Google rewards it.** Businesses that respond to reviews rank better in local search and convert more. This is a real outcome, not a manufactured one.

## Target niche (pick ONE, do not mix)

| Priority | Niche | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Dental practices & orthodontists | High customer lifetime value, already buy marketing, reputation-obsessed, dense in every city |
| **2** | Med spas / aesthetic clinics | Highest margin of any local business, entirely review-driven purchase |
| **3** | Veterinary clinics | Emotional reviews, high volume, owners have no time |
| Backup | HVAC / plumbing / auto repair | High ticket, review-driven, but lower marketing sophistication |

**Do not** target restaurants (no money, too many reviews), or anything tech-adjacent (worst reply rates in the data — under 2%).

## Qualifying filter — a prospect must have ALL of:

- [ ] 25+ Google reviews
- [ ] 10+ reviews with **no owner reply**
- [ ] Rating between 3.8 and 4.7 *(5.0 means they don't need you; under 3.5 means the problem is the business, not the reviews)*
- [ ] A findable email address — not just a contact form
- [ ] Independent or small chain — **never** a franchise with corporate marketing

## Price rules

- **Never hourly.** A 3-hour delivery at $497 is a $165/hour business. Hourly gives that away.
- **Payment before work.** Stripe payment link, paid in full, then you start.
- **First 3 clients only:** you may drop to $297 one-off to get testimonials fast. Say explicitly it is a founding-client rate so the price is not anchored.
- **Never discount the monthly.** Discount the setup instead.
