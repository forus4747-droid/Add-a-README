If you are just breaking into the commercial cleaning space and you don't know how to price, I'm going to try to break it down for you in 60 seconds. Right off the bat when pricing, you just want to avoid a couple mistakes. One, pricing by square foot. And two, itemizing all of your cleaning. Because if you itemize like "hey, $5 for bathrooms, $10 for rooms, $4 for trash receptacles", then you are legitimately screaming that you are just a commodity.

And when you are a commodity, you are making yourself no different than anyone else. So as long as you're not making those mistakes, you are off to a great start. Essentially how I've always priced — and I learned this from my franchise days, which have helped me quite a bit in my journey — the way I essentially price is by hours. Just trying to figure out how many hours, being in that office on the walkthrough, how many cleaners I'm going to need, how long it's going to take. And once I have a good understanding of my labor from there, I'm just building in my profit, my supplies, etc.
